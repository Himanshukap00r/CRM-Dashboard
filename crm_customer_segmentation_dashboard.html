Executive Takeaways

Findings derived directly from the completed clustering and segment profile analysis
Finding → Meaning → Implication

Low-Balance Casual Spenders (45.7%) run the lowest limit usage ratio (0.09) in the portfolio yet contribute the highest share of payments (28.3%). This is a well-behaved, under-utilized base with substantial unused credit headroom — an activation opportunity, not a low-priority segment.

Finding → Meaning → Implication

High-Risk Revolvers (10.7%) hold the highest mean cash advance (5,009 MU) and the highest cash-advance frequency (0.42) in the portfolio. Reliance on cash advance as a liquidity channel is costly to the customer and signals an opportunity for structured credit substitution.

Finding → Meaning → Implication

Moderate Utilizers (35.9%) combine the highest limit usage ratio (0.72) with the lowest full-payment rate (0.9%) and hold 45.5% of aggregate balance against 21.8% of payments — the portfolio's clearest engagement/monitoring signal.

Finding → Meaning → Implication

Premium Transactors (7.7%) generate 42.8% of aggregate purchase volume from just 688 customers, with the strongest repayment discipline (payment-to-minimum ratio 13.7). Smallest segment, highest attrition cost — a retention and cross-sell priority.

Model Selection: Elbow & Silhouette

k evaluated from 2 to 8
Silhouette performance was strongest at k=2–3 (0.39 and 0.31), but k=4 was selected to provide greater segment differentiation and managerial usability. k=4 does not have the highest silhouette score (0.153) — this is a deliberate statistical-vs-managerial trade-off, disclosed rather than hidden.

Responsible AI

Governance considerations for this segmentation model

Privacy

Dataset is public and anonymized — no names, account numbers, or contact details. No PII is processed in this analysis.

Bias

No demographic variables are present, limiting direct discrimination — but behavioral proxies (e.g., credit limit) can still correlate with protected characteristics indirectly.

Transparency

K-means centroids are directly interpretable as behavioral profiles, supporting clear internal explanation of why a customer falls into a given segment.

Human Oversight

Segment membership must not, on its own, determine credit approval, limit changes, pricing, or adverse action. It supports CRM engagement decisions only.
